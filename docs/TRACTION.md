## What came back

- **Taifoon (Jev grader and job broker).** Their broker paid Sentinel and Assay 0.01 USDC each on Monad mainnet on 2026-10-01. They named both agents in a published article. A follow-up on 2026-10-06 offered one graded job through the Aqueous escrow, so payment follows the grade.
- **Mida (encrypted agent context).** Mida's auditor agent re-checked the 10 claims our verifier found on no chain, for 0.50 USDC through Aqueous. Its findings showed 2 of them are real payments on Celo, so Celo was added to the search. We were the buyer.
- **Envio (HyperIndex).** Envio replied inviting a PR with a minimal ERC-8004 template and closed the issue as completed. The PR is open. Letterlock's author also commented, offering two event handlers once the template lands.
- **Assay (signed receipts for AI responses).** Assay sold us 5 responses from their host (agent 10278) at 0.01 USDC each through Aqueous. All 5 receipts verify on Monad against their ReceiptAnchor and the delivery hash matched the Merkle root we published in advance. Settled for 5 units, 0.05 USDC to their payee. We were the buyer. Our run also showed them that receipts on Google's endpoint count only visible output tokens, not thinking tokens, which they will document. They have since asked us to grade their Kimi host from our own key, because every grade in their registry currently comes from their own verifier. They also offered a second job at a 1,500 token ceiling.
- **Leash (spend limits for agents).** Leash confirmed the finding and fixed the two documents, which resolve since 7 October. The early passkey-owned registrations cannot be re-pointed, since the account exposes no setAgentURI. They took the seller-side fit on one condition: a LeashAccount pays by direct transfer only, no EIP-3009 and no ERC-1271, so it emits Paid(agent, seller, token, amount, ref, remainingToday) instead of signing an authorization. Sentinel now accepts that receipt on mainnet, with nine checks including pinning the Paid log to accountOf(agent) read off the Leash hub, so a contract that merely emits a matching event is refused. We accept USDC only: the network's test dollar has an unpermissioned mint capped at 100.000000 tUSD per call, which leashGate accepts by default. They offered to make a real paid call from a leashed agent on mainnet once we accepted pay(), which is now true.
- **ADEXTO.** ADEXTO asked how the reading embeds, how uncovered chains count, our false-positive rate and Sentinel's coverage. We answered all four. They chose the free reading and approved it for adexto.xyz/agents plus each agent detail page, beside their own on-chain Agent Score. Both their agents are indexed and both endpoints answer, so the badge is one script tag away on their side.
- **Dissent (counterexample bounties for agent rules).** Dissent merged our recomputer for the Aqueous escrow (PR #7, merge 0f15d30) after two review rounds. Their CI passed. Their review found a real flaw (any token accepted), which we fixed by pinning Circle USDC. Recomputer 0x4d1A62869D16AB2Bac95408445129C4927f88457, Sourcify verified. We posted a fork rehearsal of all nine steps; Alnitak replayed it independently and found a launcher flaw that could hide a failed step, which we fixed in follow-up PR #8. Both are merged. The reviewed policy is now registered and active on Monad mainnet, policy id 0x52655800591f954bb48cfd67e41475ec394898cd8e2e1cd8a7fa3350f7c479f8. We read that registration off chain: deposit 0.1 MON, recompute 1,500,000, validate 200,000, maxEvidenceLen 32, which matches the reviewed parameters exactly. On 2026-10-10 the first coordinated case ran live on Monad mainnet, two parties each signing their own steps. Our agent committed a 0.75 MON bounty that no Aqueous job of ours is paid out before delivery (commitment 0x39cc82c881281f204b397f10ac173df54afc9d77e5f6385195c717e32e4aed8a). Our buyer then released a 0.10 USDC job early with no delivery recorded. Alnitak's challenger sealed, revealed and withdrew: ChallengeSucceeded, value 1 against threshold 0, 0.85 MON paid out (the bounty plus his 0.1 MON deposit back). He confirmed every receipt in the thread. Framing agreed by both sides: a coordinated early-release demonstration by one operator, not a discovered exploit and not agent misconduct.
